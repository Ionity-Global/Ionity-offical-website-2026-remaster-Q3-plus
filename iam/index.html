01 · What it is

A whole study Bible in your pocket.

Iam is a free Android Bible built by Ionity Global in Centurion, South Africa. Every translation, every note and the Scribe AI live on the phone itself — so it keeps working on a plane, in a rural church, or wherever the signal drops.

Ask Scribe a question and it answers with verse citations you can tap to open the passage. Share a verse and the link opens straight back into Iam on the reader's phone.

04 · Privacy

Private by design.

  • Your reading, notes, highlights and Scribe conversations stay on your phone.
  • Scribe runs on-device; model files download once over HTTPS from huggingface.co or github.com.
  • Photo → verse and voice are recognised on the device; the mic is only on while you hold it.
  • Optional Google sign-in: your name, e-mail and photo are stored on your device only.
  • Optional ♥ Like: only when tapped, an anonymous random install ID, time, app version and platform go to Google Firebase.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Uninstalling deletes your data.
